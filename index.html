PROOF LEVELS Class [C1]–[C5]
[C1] Direct Fact Video pixels, timestamps & on-screen text
[C2] Brand Rule Company brand book, safe-zones & legal claims
[C3] Past Data Historical performance benchmarks
[C4] AI Estimate Projected engagement & pacing models
[C5] Missing No source found — pauses approval
CORE RULES Decision Safety
1. Source-Backed Scores: Every recommendation must cite an exact timestamp or rule.
2. Pause on Uncertainty: Missing proof locks budget release until manually checked.
3. Fast Sign-Off: Replaces Slack debates with a clear, 1-page decision report.
